# Vademecum of the arts (1050–1510)

A pocket timeline of painting, sculpture, architecture, and music — Name, years, place; Vasari when he wrote; external listen links for music.

Extends from Romanesque work of the mid-eleventh century through artists **born ≤1454** (Leonardo da Vinci in; Michelangelo/Raphael out). Timeline works may run into the early 1500s. Vasari’s Lives begin with Cimabue; earlier Italian and Low Countries figures, plus a few neighbours for context, fill 1050–1250.

## Use

- Century buttons jump the scroll (11th–15th c.); the strip itself stays ordered by year.
- Tap a colored line for the full floruit highlight + works / Vasari / listen links.
- Color key filters a medium; tap again or empty parchment to clear.

## Local

Open `index.html`, or `python3 -m http.server 8766`.
