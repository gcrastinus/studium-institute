# Notes

## Layout
Vertical gallery by decade (not a narrow accordion fold). Large cards; empty medium tracks are hidden.

## Scope
Italy and the Low Countries, 1050–1510 (extension: artists born ≤1454; see NOTES-extension.md). The 1050–1250 stretch is mostly named Italian and Mosan/Low Countries figures; a few French and German works (Saint-Denis, Chartres west façade, Gislebertus, Hildegard) sit in for context. A sparse “Ancient models” row holds monuments this generation looked at (Pisa Phaedra sarcophagus, Marcus Aurelius at the Lateran, Arch of Constantine) — not modern excavations like Pompeii.

## Vasari
Public-domain excerpts from Gaston du C. de Vere, Vols. I–IV (Project Gutenberg #25326, #25759, #26860, #28420). Artists with a dedicated Life also get an external link to the complete text after the work excerpts. Duccio has a Life (Vol. II) even without excerpts here. Coppo, Guido, Torriti, and the Magdalen Master have no dedicated Life (or only a note).

## Skipped or deferred
- Frederick II Capua gate head: no color ≥800×800 Commons file found.
- Maastricht Hours and other Low Countries illuminations: Commons folios were below 800×800 or rate-limited.
- Cologne choir / Utrecht Dom / Brussels Gudula: first Commons hits were wrong subjects (book cover, engraving, modern statue) and were discarded.
- Ypres Cloth Hall: post-WWI reconstruction — omitted rather than mislead.
- Santa Croce neo-Gothic façade omitted; Brunelleschi’s dome now included in the extension.
- Cimabue Assisi Crucifixion is shown in its oxidized “negative” state.

## Images
All JPEGs verified color with both dimensions ≥800. Data is inlined in `app.js` so double-click / `file://` works without a server.
