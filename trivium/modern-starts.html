A dialectical entrance

Where do you want to begin?

❧

Most readers do not arrive empty. They arrive with a reasonable modern preference about what should come first in an education. The five paths below take those preferences seriously. Each grants what is true in the impulse, then shows what becomes incoherent if words are treated as secondary, and then returns to a shared foundation.

This is not a skill drill and not a quiz. It is a short dialectic. Choose the sentence that sounds most like you. You can return and take another path later; the contents will remember the last one you chose.

Science and data

We should start from measurement and evidence, not from words.

Critical thinking

We should start from logic puzzles and skip grammar.

Voice and self-expression

We should start from having something to say, and fix language later.

Words as labels

Words are just names stuck on things; the things are what matter.

Machines that write

If a machine can draft the sentences, why train grammar and rhetoric?

If none of these is your objection, go on to Why we begin with words.
